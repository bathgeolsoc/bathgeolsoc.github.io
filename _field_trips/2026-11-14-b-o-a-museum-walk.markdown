**10am-10:45am: Bradford on Avon Museum**
It is a small museum packed with interesting objects that tell the story of the local area, both its natural history and industrial heritage. Of special interest to geologists will be the local crinoid fossils and information on the underground stone quarries. The Museum has a gift shop.

**11:00am-12:45pm:** After visiting the museum, we will take a short walk around Bradford on Avon visiting some of the sites of geological interest; building stones, quarry sites and springs. Adapted depending on the weather!

**1:00pm:** Lunch in one of the local cafés for those who wish to stay longer.
