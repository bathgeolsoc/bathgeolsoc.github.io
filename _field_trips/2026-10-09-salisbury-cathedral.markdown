Steve Hanneth from the Wiltshire Geology Group and Salisbury Cathedral has kindly arranged a special tour of the cathedral and the Mason’s Workshop on Friday, 9th October. The cathedral is famous for having the tallest spire in the UK and rests on compacted river gravel, making its foundations unique. The building stones comprise sandy limestones of the Portland Formation, fossiliferous Purbeck “Marble” and Bathonian Caen stone taken from the ruins of the 11th century seat of the Bishops of Winchester at Old Sarum. This promises be a very fascinating day. Unfortunately for those with work commitments this exclusive access is only available on week day.

11.00 Meet at the front of the Salisbury Museum which is right opposite the Cathedral entrance. Walk round the Close for interpretation walk on the earth materials used in the different constructions around the Close.

12.15 Lunch - Cathedral cafe is good or lots in Salisbury - close by there is a Cafe also in Museum and seating in the Close for a picnic if weather good.

1.40 Tour of Masons' Workshops

All walking is on firm ground with no gradients. The tour is of the Close is outside but Masons' Tour is mainly under cover.

There is no charge as Steve has been allowed a guide freebie for friends and family. However each attendee is kindly asked to make a donation (we suggest £10) to the Cathedral which will be collected beforehand (the charge for the tour is normally £20).
